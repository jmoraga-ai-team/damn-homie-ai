Brain dump in. Game plan out.

Damn Homie AI is a helper for people whose brains run fast and messy. You talk or type everything out of your head. It sorts it into a short list of next steps, and remembers you next time.

Set it upHow your data is handled

Who it is for

Chaotic smart people. Neurodivergent brains (ADHD and friends). Anyone who has ten tabs open in their head and wants the next one thing.

What it does

  • Save Point: your quests, next steps and wins, loaded at the start of every chat.
  • My Rules: how you like answers: short, numbered, one step at a time.
  • Projects: a one-page summary plus a details doc for each big thing.
  • Daily tidy: finished items move to an Archive so your list stays short.
  • Safety Homie: it will not save keys, passwords, card numbers or ID numbers, and it asks before anything risky.

Your memory is yours

Everything it remembers lives in a folder called Damn Homie in your own Google Drive. We only keep the sign-in key, locked. You can read, edit or delete your files any time.

Works with

Claude, ChatGPT and Grok. Same memory in all three.
