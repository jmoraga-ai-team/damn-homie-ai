Privacy Policy

Draft. Written by an AI, not a lawyer. It says in plain words what the app really does. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

The short version

  • Your notes and memory live in your own Google Drive, in a folder called Damn Homie.
  • Our server keeps only a locked sign-in grant. No name, no email, no notes.
  • We do not sell, share or read your data.

Who we are

Damn Homie AI is run by Jarod Moraga in Maui, Hawaii. Contact: roomforopinions@gmail.com.

What you give Google permission to do

When you sign in with Google, you allow one thing: the drive.file permission. It lets Damn Homie AI see and change only the files it created (or files you pick for it). It cannot see the rest of your Drive. We also get a basic sign-in ID from Google. We do not ask for your email, name, contacts or calendar.

What we store on our server

  • Your Google access token and refresh token. They are encrypted by the sign-in library, with a key tied to your own token.
  • A one-way hash of your Google account ID, so the app knows it is you next time. It cannot be turned back into your ID.

That is all. We store no chats, no Save Point, no file contents, no name and no email.

What happens to what you say

Your words go to the AI you chose (Claude, ChatGPT or Grok), under that company's own rules. When the AI saves a memory, our server passes it straight to your Google Drive and does not keep a copy. Before anything is written, the app blanks things that look like API keys, passwords, ID numbers and card numbers.

Google API Limited Use

Damn Homie AI's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to run your memory inside the app. We do not use it for ads, sell it, or let people read it. We do not use it to train AI models.

How to delete everything

  1. Remove the connector in your AI app.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Damn Homie AI. This cancels our access.
  3. Delete the Damn Homie folder in your Google Drive to erase your memory.
  4. To also erase the locked sign-in grant we hold, email us and we will delete it.

Kids

Damn Homie AI is not for children under 13.

Changes

If this changes we will update the date above. Questions: roomforopinions@gmail.com.
